Research Interests

The threads running through my research — where geospatial data science meets the everyday reality of climate risk.

Flood Susceptibility & Exposure Modeling

Machine learning (XGBoost, LightGBM, ANN) applied to conditioning factors to map where and why floods hit hardest.

Geospatial Machine Learning

GIS, remote sensing, and predictive modeling combined to turn raw spatial data into decision-ready risk maps.

Coastal Community Resilience

Community disaster resilience indices built with PCA and AHP to benchmark adaptive capacity in coastal Bangladesh.

Gender & Climate Displacement

Documenting the lived, non-economic losses of climate-displaced women in cyclone-prone unions like Gabura.

Disaster Risk Reduction Policy

Translating exposure and vulnerability findings into planning recommendations for flood-risk-informed land use.

Sustainable Materials & Circular Economy

Life-cycle assessment of jute-based alternatives to plastic, linking environmental planning to material science.

Featured Projects

M.Sc. Thesis · Lead Researcher

Flood Susceptibility & Exposure Assessment, Bangladesh

Comparative machine-learning modeling of flood susceptibility across the central and south-western floodplains, benchmarking tree-based and neural network approaches against eleven geospatial conditioning factors.

  • Built and compared XGBoost, LightGBM, and Artificial Neural Network models using TWI, slope, rainfall, elevation, land cover, and drainage-distance predictors.
  • Ran multicollinearity diagnostics (correlation matrix, VIF) to refine the conditioning-factor set.
  • Produced exposure maps to guide flood-risk-informed land-use planning; manuscript under review.
Research Assistant / Analyst

Female Vulnerability in Gabura, Satkhira

A comprehensive study focusing on the female demographic in the cyclone-prone Gabura union, investigating socio-economic and environmental impacts.

  • Conducted field surveys and qualitative interviews with women in the area.

  • Developed planning recommendations for women's empowerment and disaster risk reduction.
